The Sanctuary for Natural Heritage

A place where nature, science, and architecture converge to preserve the Earth's hidden and living heritage.

Concept Statement

What lies beneath and what lives above coexist in a delicate balance. The earth below nurtures the life above, while the life above enriches and restores what lies beneath. To sustain one, we must protect both, for together they sustain each other in an endless cycle of coexistence.
