A Collection of Hand-Drawn Architectural Works

Multiverse

This project explored creativity through the idea of adding a fictional element to an existing home. I chose my house in Bsalim and imagined an addition that acts as a gateway to a parallel universe existing alongside our own. In this alternate world, parallel versions of the people from the real house exist, sharing the same identity but living different experiences shaped by different choices and circumstances. The concept creates a connection between reality and imagination, exploring how one person could exist in multiple versions across different worlds.
