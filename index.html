– Village of Balaa, Batroun District

A natural sciences complex with a museum, research lab, water treatment facility, workshops, theater, restaurant, and dormitory spaces. Located near the unfinished Balaa Dam, the project extends its context while taking inspiration from the Baatara Gorge.

Design 6
Transportation Museum
– Batroun Old Souk

Museum in the Old Souk that presents the evolution of mobility, from the former railway and train to tuk-tuks, golf carts, and electric vehicles. The project connects this history with Batroun’s present-day circulation through a sequence of exhibition spaces, a digital area, café, and gift shop.

Elderly Housing & Community Center
– Abd El Wahab Street, Beirut

A mixed-use project that combines housing for retired elderly people with a community center open to everyone. Its shared communal garden brings different generations together through gardening, social activities, and everyday interaction.

About me

Welcome to my portfolio. I’m Magalie, an honors Architecture graduate with a Minor in Business. Most of my projects were developed in Revit, helping me build strong skills in BIM modeling, documentation, technical drawings, and project coordination.

Through internships, site experience, design-build workshops, and executed projects, I have learned how to carry an idea from concept through development and construction. I also have strong skills in architectural visualization, rendering, and VR presentation. I am creative, detail-oriented, adaptable, and always open to learning new tools, exploring new ideas, and taking on new challenges.
