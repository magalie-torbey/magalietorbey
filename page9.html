C.V

Lighting Installation

This lighting installation, developed as part of a university project, explored how light can shape the perception and atmosphere of an interior space. Through experimenting with reflection, transmission, shadows, and color, I learned how light can transform the character of a room, influence its mood, and completely change the way a space is experienced.
